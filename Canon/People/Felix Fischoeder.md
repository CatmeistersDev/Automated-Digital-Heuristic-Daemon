# 👤 Felix Fischoeder

Younger brother of [[Mr. Fischoeder]], temperamental, and bankrolled entirely by him — a favor Calvin repays himself by saying out loud, often, that Felix cannot do anything right on his own. Felix is also the one who took his brother's eye out. He wants [[Wonder Wharf]] for himself, which makes him useful and unreliable in the same breath.

Louise keeps him outside the crew and outside the plan for [[Make the Funtime raid a decoy]]. He is also the reason a bare "Fischoeder" in a memo means nothing on its own.
